# Code of Conduct

This is a small project, and we'd like it to stay a friendly one.

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent; disagree about code, not about people.
- Welcome newcomers and people whose first language isn't yours. Issues and PRs may be written in
  English or Japanese, and imperfect language is never a problem.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, or sharing of others' private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive. "This doesn't fit the project" is a valid answer;
  it is not a judgement of the person who asked.

If someone is making the project unpleasant, contact the maintainer privately (via GitHub) —
reports are handled discreetly. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove content and block accounts
that don't follow these guidelines.
